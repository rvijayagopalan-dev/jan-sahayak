Legal information is not legal advice. Verify current commencement, amendments, State rules, jurisdiction and facts. No automated legal determination, investigation, adjudication or filing is performed.

Core criminal law framework

India's three new criminal laws
Act 45 of 2023

BNS

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita — substantive criminal offences, liability and punishments.

Act 46 of 2023

BNSS

Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita — criminal procedure, investigation, arrest, bail and trial.

Act 47 of 2023

BSA

Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam — relevance, admissibility and proof of evidence.

Core architecture principles

Federated, not centralized authority

Systems of record remain with their accountable institutions.

API-first, handoff-ready

Use standards-based adapters where authorized; transparent fallback where not.

Privacy by design

Purpose limitation, data minimization, consent, segregation and audit.

Human-supervised agents

Bounded permissions, evaluation, traceability and escalation.

Accessible by default

Multilingual, voice-first, low-bandwidth and disability-inclusive.

Open and portable

Versioned contracts, data portability and reduced vendor lock-in.
